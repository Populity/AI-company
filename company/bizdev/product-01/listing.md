## Full description
Meet Walt. He has been at his desk since 1987. He has attended every meeting, filed every form, and has never taken a day off. He made these templates to keep the paperwork polite.

**Ghost-Approved Office Templates** is a 10-piece pack of printable and fillable humor templates for anyone who works in an office, a home office, or an office that is mostly a calendar.

**What you get**
1. Form 27-B: Day Off Request, the form Walt has been trying to get approved for 39 years
2. The Memo That Replaces This Meeting
3. Polite Out-of-Office for the Eternally Busy (5 versions)
4. Clock In / Clock Out: a weekly planner
5. The Jargon-to-Human Dictionary (20 office phrases, translated)
6. The "Could This Have Been a Memo?" Checklist, with scoring
7. Form 14-D: Respectful Meeting Decline
8. Per My Last Memo: a follow-up message generator
9. The Honest Daily Timesheet
10. Certificate of Survived Meeting

Every page has a short note in Walt's dry voice and a Gable Stamp Box. Leave it empty. He won't notice. Probably.

**Good for**
- A gift or office joke for a colleague
- Printing and pinning above your desk
- Copying the message templates into your email
- Fans of the Still Clocked In series on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels
